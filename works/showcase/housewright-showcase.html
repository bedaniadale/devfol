The problem

Most people can't picture a plan.

A homeowner with an idea has two bad options: a paper sketch that never looks like a house, or professional CAD that takes weeks to learn. Housewright sits in between.

"Will the sofa fit if we knock this wall through? And what will it look like from the garden at dusk?"

Sketches lie

Hand-drawn plans drift off scale, so a 12′ room on paper turns out to be 10′ in real life.

CAD overwhelms

Pro tools ask about layers, wall types and section cuts before you can place a bed.

2D hides the feel

A floor plan can't show light, height or how rooms open onto each other.

Accounts get in the way

Sign-up walls and installs lose the person who just wanted to try an idea.

The renovator

Has a tape measure and a house that almost works. Types in the real room sizes, then moves one wall at a time.

"Show me the kitchen without that wall."

The first-home buyer

Picking between floor plans before meeting a builder. Starts from a template and changes the layout.

"Do two bedrooms fit upstairs?"

The dreamer

No plot yet, just a picture in mind. Starts on a blank lot and walks through the result on a Sunday afternoon.

"I want to stand in it."
Key features

Everything a house needs, nothing a studio needs.

Every screen keeps the house in the middle. The library sits on the left, settings on the right, and tools hide until you need them.

Start fast

Five modern houses, ready on day one.

New design opens with real, editable starter homes, each with its own rooms, finishes and garden. Or start from a blank lot and draw it yourself.

Plan to scale

Drop rooms in at real sizes.

Each room in the library comes with a sensible size: Bedroom 12′0″ × 12′0″, Kitchen 12′0″ × 10′0″, Living room 16′0″ × 14′0″. Drag it in and pull its walls to your measurements.

  • Floors. Ground floor, Upper floor, Add floor, or see All floors side by side.
  • Live totals. 1,196 sq ft living · 1 bath, updated as you draw.
  • Doors, windows, stairs and fixtures snap to walls.
One house, two views

Change the plan, watch the house change.

Split puts the dimensioned plan next to the 3D model. They read from the same data, so a wall moved on the left moves on the right, to the inch.

Walk around it

Five ways to look at your home.

  • Edit. Walk inside and arrange furniture.
  • Dollhouse. Look into the rooms from above.
  • Orbit. Circle around the house.
  • Walk. Walk through at eye height.
  • Tour. An automatic fly-around.
Furnish it

A library you can browse like a shop.

Furniture, garden and garage pieces live in plain groups: Sofas (13 items), Beds (17), Dining tables (9), Garden (15). Search "Search furniture, garden, garage" and drag a piece into the room. Dollhouse mode cuts the walls Low, Half or Full so you can see inside.

Light it

Any hour, from sunrise to night.

A single dial sets the time of day: Morning, Noon, Golden hour, Dusk and Night. At night the house lights come on, with switches per room.

User flow

From idea to walkthrough in five steps.

The happy path a first-time visitor takes. No sign-up screen sits anywhere in it.

  1. 1

    Open Your designs

    "Design a dream home, start from a modern template, or redraw your own house to its real sizes."

  2. 2

    Name it, pick a start

    Choose one of five templates or a blank lot, then Create design.

  3. 3

    Shape the plan

    Add rooms, pull walls to size, place doors and windows, add a floor.

  4. 4

    Furnish in 3D

    Switch to 3D, drag furniture in, check it from Dollhouse and Walk.

  5. 5

    Render and present

    Pick the hour, make a Photo render or Tour video, then Present.

What's next

The roadmap.

Housewright ships in plans, each one working software. The foundation, catalog and 3D editing are in. These come next.

Next

Real measurements

Metric by default, inside-clear room sizes, per-wall thickness, ceiling heights and a "My own house" start flow.

Later

Building elements

Stair types and railings, window and door styles, curtains, balconies, skylights and cladding per wall.

Later

Plan export

PNG and PDF per floor with dimensions, scale bar, north arrow and a title block at true scale on A4 and A3.

Later

Accounts

Optional sign-in and cloud saving, so a design can move between devices. Today it lives in your browser.
